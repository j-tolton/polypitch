Controls

Drag
Scroll through time or pitch
Wheel
Scroll pitch · Shift: time
Ctrl+wheel
Zoom pitch · Alt+wheel: zoom time
Keys
Arrows scroll · + − zoom · End: back to live
Touch
One finger scrolls · pinch zooms pitch · tap reads a note
Listen
Starts the microphone. Stop, then Listen again, carries on the same trace; Clear starts afresh
Play
Plays back what was heard, from where you tap or from the start of the view · Space starts and stops it · the bar below shows the whole session, green where it was in tune
Follow
On whenever you start or clear. Dragging the pitch view turns it off until then; tap Follow to turn it back on
Instrument
Frames its range, shades the rest and ignores anything below its lowest note. Trumpet, baritone, clarinet and saxophones can name notes as written on their music, or at concert pitch
Tuner
The tuning fork button switches to the tuner, which always listens and keeps nothing. Switching to it clears the trace; back in the trace, press Listen to start again. Chromatic shows the nearest note; Strings shows each string and ticks the ones in tune. Tap a string to tune only that one, or Edit to change a tuning. Strum reads every string at once: strum them all and let them ring for a second, then fine-tune any that are out on Strings
App
Add to Home Screen from your browser's menu to run it full screen like an app. It works offline once it has been opened online.
